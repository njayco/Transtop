# Intercity GTFS coverage audit

Reviewed **October 30, 2026**. This is an audit, not authorization to publish current departures. No app, database, workflow, environment or license-acceptance changes were made for this audit. The national research-reference integration already present from the separate earlier request is not evidence of current service or reuse permission.

## Decision

**No archived provider subset is ready to become verified current departure results.** Use this archive for carrier discovery and research only until freshness, precise boarding points, calendar exceptions and applicable reuse terms are independently established. Prioritize fresh authorized upstream sources rather than repairing dates on the national snapshot.

The New York-area screen finds **17 source agencies, 248 directional route IDs and 1,069 archived trip patterns**. These are candidate records, not daily departures or confirmed active services. A known wrong-city coordinate means even geographical inclusion requires revalidation.

Most are 2025, some January 2026, and some national rows are older still. - GTFS: 72 agencies, 1,044 routes, 3,673 trips, 3,919 stops, **26,757 stop times, 512 weekly calendars and 85 calendar exceptions**. Eight files; no frequencies.txt, feed_info.txt or realtime observations. Calendar spans include 2010–2035. Shapes alone are 118,941,124 uncompressed bytes.

- Regional screen: raw stop coordinates inside latitude **40.45–41.10**, longitude **−74.30–−71.80** (NYC/Long Island, nearby NJ and coastal southwest Connecticut). Join stop_times → trips → routes → agency; retain full ordered trip sequences and connected endpoints, including outside the box. This is an explicit NY-first seed screen, **not** a Connecticut-wide or national completeness claim.
- [All-provider matrix](intercity-gtfs-provider-matrix.csv) covers all 72 source agencies, dates, URL provenance, calendar envelopes, exceptions, terms and readiness. [Candidate trip index](intercity-gtfs-regional-trips.csv) identifies every screened trip/service/route and its ordered stop IDs. Zero screened trips means outside this candidate set, not no service anywhere.

## Genuine potential coverage gains

Relative to the original supplied terminal-directory inventory, **Concord Coach Lines, Superior Tours, Ocean Travel and Best Bus** are additional candidate operators. They serve curbside as well as terminal locations; none should be forced into a Port Authority hub. Specific candidate corridors include NYC–Portland/Concord, NYC–Pikesville/Baltimore, NYC–Buffalo/Rochester/Liverpool/Binghamton, and NYC–DC/Virginia/Delaware. These are discovery gains, not approved current timetable imports.

Existing link-only providers gain reference leads rather than new verified departures: Peter Pan (NYC–Boston/Hartford/Philadelphia/DC/Providence), C&J (NYC–New Hampshire), ShortLine (Hudson Valley/Woodbury Common), Trailways subsidiaries (Albany/Catskills/upstate/Canada), Fullington (Pennsylvania), Academy-labelled Go Buses patterns, and Greyhound/FlixBus regional/national connections. The trip index retains exact encoded endpoints and stop sequences rather than fabricating through-services.

Bonanza/BZ maps to Peter Pan. FlixBus/Greyhound share a parent/feed family, but do not deduplicate physical trips solely by parent ownership or stop-name equality. Keep distinct Adirondack/New York/Pine Hill operating identities until actual interlining is verified. The separately named Ohio GoBus is not automatically Academy's Go Buses. Retain provider+source namespaces; renumbered research IDs are not stable upstream IDs.

## Separate assessments by data type

### Stops
Coordinates are research orientation points, not verified boarding poles/gates. Publisher processing uses fuzzy stop reduction and snapping to shapes within 100 m. Identical names can identify different locations. In the actual GTFS, **State College Regional Airport** occurs as raw stop **1#2382 at 40.734062, −74.165535 (Newark area)** and **2382 at 40.85141, −77.8528 (Pennsylvania)**. This is a material name/location inconsistency, not ordinary 100 m snapping. Exclude/quarantine suspicious points from rider nearby claims until carrier verification; do not silently move a pin based on name guesses.

Transtop's reviewed location decisions in `artifacts/api-server/src/transit/intercity/ibg-verification.ts` bind this quarantine to the pinned archive's version, SHA-256 and raw stop `1#2382`. Each serving provider retains its own namespaced identity. The raw snapshot and compiled archival stop map retain the original coordinates and audit evidence; the boarding catalog, nearby provider discovery, network points, route boarding selectors and historical boarding rows omit the quarantined record. Coverage messages and affected route/provider warnings explain the omission, even when no eligible points remain. Raw stop `2382` stays eligible as a **research reference**, not an independently verified boarding location. Same names and unrelated carrier records are not suppression rules.

To release a quarantined record, obtain independent carrier/boarding-location evidence for that exact source point and review a `verified` decision recording the evidence URL, check date and notes. Reimporting the same archive, observing a future calendar end, or comparing two research records is not independent verification. A decision never substitutes another stop's coordinates. Any corrected location from a new source needs its own reviewed source record; when updating the pinned archive, review whether existing findings still apply rather than blindly carrying raw IDs across versions.

Current official evidence also proves date-specific changes matter: Concord's [October 31 notice](https://concordcoachlines.com/nyc-temporary-stop-change-october-31-2026/) moves only its 08:00 NYC trip to 370 Lexington Avenue. [C&J](https://www.ridecj.com/new-york-city) names Gate 36 and reservations; [Greyhound](https://www.greyhound.com/bus/new-york-ny/new-york-port-authority) confirms 625 8th Ave while making tickets authoritative. None validates all archived stop coordinates.

### Route geometry
Article explicitly says some broken/missing shapes were replaced using Google Maps directions. Notebook also snaps stops and simplifies/merges shapes. GeoJSON is useful for approximate research visualization, not proof of the exact carrier road path or a vehicle observation. A legal and provenance review is needed before reproducing synthesized geometry. Do not fill the three missing GeoJSON routes with invented lines or count 1,988 line features as 1,988 routes.

### Schedules and timezones
All actual agency timezones are America/New_York, including western operators. Notebook documents standardizing timezone metadata; this does not establish correctly converted local clocks. Preserve native agency and stop timezone semantics when obtaining fresh sources, including extended GTFS hours and service-day/DST handling. Some route names were assigned by researchers. Source dates and future calendar ends do not establish current service. ShortLine's encoded calendar expired August 2025; Greyhound/FlixBus expire May/June 2026. [ShortLine's current page](https://www.coachusa.com/shortline/schedules) explicitly warns of limited schedules and no Long Island service until further notice, contradicting any blanket reinstatement from older records.

### Holidays, exceptions and frequency
Notebook removes start_date=end_date services before weekly frequency calculation, including related exception/trip/timepoint rows. Remaining 85 exception records cannot demonstrate complete holiday coverage. Most regional agencies have **zero** exception rows. Preserve existing Trans-Bridge November 26–27, 2026 replacements and both-direction Wall Street closure; never substitute the archive's generic weekdays. Current [Concord schedules change October 25, 2026](https://concordcoachlines.com/new-schedules-october-25-2026/) and holiday advisories further demonstrate why the 2025 snapshot needs replacement. GeoJSON weekly_trips is an analytical aggregate, not a headway-based rider schedule.

### Live tracking
Neither archive contains GTFS-RT vehicle positions, observation timestamps, trip updates or tracking authorization. **No provider is ready for live GPS through this dataset.** Static paths, scheduled time interpolation and frequency estimates must not become moving buses. Existing real observations from other transport sources are unaffected.

## Rights and attribution

No root dataset LICENSE was found at the reviewed revision. The article's CC BY-SA 4.0 license does not automatically establish rights to underlying TDS, manually copied, operator, or Google-derived data. This is an access/authorization assessment, not a legal determination that public timetable facts are categorically unusable.

- **TDS-sourced rows:** public map availability and historical date selectors establish discoverability, not a redistribution or recurring scraping grant. Obtain applicable data-owner terms/authorization.
- **Transitland/Mobility Database rows:** registry entries identify leads; the carrier's source terms must still be checked. [Transitland terms](https://www.transit.land/terms) explicitly place source-license compliance on data/API consumers; software licensing is separate.
- **Manual rows:** researcher transcription does not establish the carrier's distribution grant. Confirm factual reuse and collection terms before a fresh automated integration.
- **Peter Pan:** [legal index](https://peterpanbus.com/legal-notices/) was updated October 5, 2026. [General terms](https://peterpanbus.com/general-terms-of-service/) restrict scraping/data extraction and redistribution without written consent and describe personal/noncommercial access. Do not build a booking scraper absent separate applicable authorization. No terms were accepted and no bypass was attempted.
- All proposed imports need named upstream provenance, source date, fetch/publication dates, permitted-use basis and source-specific required credit. Credit Ahammed, Shih and Lehe, the article DOI and repository; retain any upstream notices. If reproducing/adapting article-licensed material, meet CC BY-SA attribution/change/share-alike requirements for that material without asserting that those terms settle every underlying feed.

## Smallest safe follow-on scope

1. Keep the existing location/mode/provider experience, no separate terminal hub, no national auto-upgrade into current predictions. An already displayed research label does not resolve rights or wrong-city pins.
2. First resolve the research snapshot's applicable distribution terms and quarantine material coordinate inconsistencies. Keep newer Martz and Trans-Bridge primary records untouched.
3. For a first **new current provider**, evaluate Superior Tours' public NYC line-run page: a small independently described corridor with visible factual stop/time tables. Obtain an applicable reuse basis, confirm weekday qualifiers and holiday exceptions, verify NYC pickup location, and then validate each trip pattern. Until those blockers clear, link to the official page only. Concord offers a second candidate but requires temporary-stop/holiday handling; Peter Pan/FlixBus need authorized upstream access rather than copying research dates or bypassing booking restrictions.
4. Import only validated trips attached to existing provider/location filters, with precise source/update/status labels. Preserve pickup/drop-off/reservation restrictions and original service days. Verify absence of duplicate aliases/interlining and wrong-city stops before publication.
5. Treat road geometry and live tracking as separate approvals/sources. Missing authorized current data must produce an explicit coverage gap, never a shifted research calendar.

**Remaining blockers:** per-source reuse authorization, fresh complete timetable/holiday evidence, verified boarding coordinates, native timezone validation, stable identity mapping and (separately) an authorized live tracking source. **Ready current-departure subsets from IBG: zero.**
