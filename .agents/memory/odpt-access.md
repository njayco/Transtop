---
name: ODPT access boundaries
description: Owner-approved scope for direct Japanese transit sources and unapproved access.
---

Direct Japanese transit must remain usable independently of Transitland. Approval
for the initial public CC BY datasets does not authorize signup, new agreements,
Challenge participation, paid entitlements, authenticated ODPT Basic feeds, or
changing Transitland's approved budget.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested current public bus/tram coverage without
an ODPT account and excluded those access changes. ODPT Basic's restrictions
cannot be inferred from the catalog's generic open-data branding.

**How to apply:** Treat each additional operator/format/terms change as a separate
review. Keep restricted sources disabled unless the owner approves their
agreements and provides access through Secrets. Never infer Tokyo Metro/JR or
complete national coverage from another Japanese operator.
