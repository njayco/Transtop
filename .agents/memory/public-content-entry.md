---
name: Public information and the entry experience
description: Why public transit information is independent of the interactive app's deliberate entry.
---

Keep read-only public transit information accessible without completing the entry gesture. Do not automatically enter the interactive app for crawlers or remove its artwork to solve indexing.

**Why:** The requested entry artwork and deliberate swipe/keyboard experience must remain, while search and social services need the same useful informational HTML that riders can read without JavaScript or session storage.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between public information and live interactive features. Treat a temporary provider failure as unavailable information, not evidence that the entity does not exist.
